Overview

// what it is

README.md

An agentic Slack assistant that acts as the first responder for any operational request coming into the infrastructure team. When engineers or stakeholders ask about runbooks, access procedures, or platform status, Echo Bot handles it autonomously.

The point is not the chatbot — it is giving the team back time for high-value engineering work instead of repetitive Q&A.
